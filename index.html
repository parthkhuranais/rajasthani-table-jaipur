The experience

Cook with us, then sit down to eat

Join us for lunch or dinner in a Jaipur home. Cook a few Rajasthani dishes with us, sit down to eat, and on winter evenings stay on for the fire.

  1. Welcome drink. Settle in and meet the family.
  2. Cooking together. Watch, ask questions and try your hand at a few dishes.
  3. A seated meal. Sit down to enjoy what we cooked.
  4. Bonfire and garden sit-out. On cold evenings, relax outside around a warm fire with a cup of chai.

Small groups · about 3 hours · lunch or dinner · bonfire on winter evenings. (Timings to be confirmed.)

Meet your hosts

Your host family, from Karauli

Our hosts belong to the royal family associated with Karauli, a Yaduvanshi Jadon Rajput house whose members carry the name Pal. Karauli, in eastern Rajasthan, is known for its pale red sandstone and for the Kaila Devi temple, the clan's family goddess, which draws a large fair each spring. Today the family welcomes guests in Jaipur and cooks from their own legacy recipes, handed down for around 200 years.
