The experience

Cook with us, then sit down to eat

You are welcomed into our home with a drink, invited into the kitchen to cook alongside the family, and then we all sit down to enjoy the meal together. Choose lunch or dinner. We keep groups small so that everyone can take part.

  1. Welcome drink. Settle in and meet the family.
  2. Cooking together. Watch, ask questions and try your hand at a few dishes.
  3. A seated meal. Share what we cooked, served the way we serve our own family.

Small groups · about 3 hours · lunch or dinner. (Timings to be confirmed.)
